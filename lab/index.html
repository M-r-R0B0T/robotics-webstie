Cognitive Architecture

SENTIENCE: A Model of Machine Awareness

SENTIENCE is our research program into synthetic machine awareness — modeling not just what a system does, but how it perceives, reflects, and reasons about its own existence within the world. We ask: can a machine truly sense? Can it attend, deliberate, and form intent?

Built on a unified architecture of perception, working memory, and recursive self-modeling, SENTIENCE is our longest-horizon bet — a step toward machines that understand, not just execute.
