ANCALAGON / RESEARCH

AI & Autonomy

Intelligence that lives in the body.

We train robots
the way humans
learn.

Our research spans foundation models for robotics, imitation learning from human demonstration, real-time motor control, and sim-to-real transfer. We believe that intelligence must be deeply grounded in the body — perception, action, and learning must be unified.

By doing, failing, and refining in the real world, our robots acquire skills that generalize across environments, objects, and tasks.

01 — RESEARCH AREAS
01

Foundation Models for Robotics

Pre-training cross-embodiment transformers on large-scale robot teleoperation datasets, enabling rapid adaptation to new tasks with minimal demonstration data.

Transformer Architecture Transfer Learning State Representation
02

Imitation Learning

Learning dexterous manipulation and locomotion policies directly from human demonstration via behavior cloning and inverse reinforcement learning.

Behavior Cloning IRL DAGGER
03

Real-Time Motor Control

High-frequency control loops at 1 kHz for reactive, compliant interaction with unstructured environments — from thin-object grasping to dynamic walking.

Model Predictive Control Whole-Body Control Contact-Rich Manipulation
04

Sim-to-Real Transfer

Domain randomization, physics-informed rendering, and adaptive environments to train policies that transfer seamlessly from simulation to the physical world.

Domain Randomization Physics Simulation Adaptive Transfer
05

Language Grounding

Bridging natural language instructions and physical robot behavior through vision-language-action models trained on paired human-robot demonstrations.

VLA Models Language Instructions Multimodal Alignment
