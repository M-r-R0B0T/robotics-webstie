ANCALAGON / VISAGE

VISAGE

A general-purpose humanoid robot
built for the real world.

01 — OVERVIEW

A body built
for the world.

VISAGE is Ancalagon Robotics' flagship general-purpose humanoid — engineered to operate alongside humans in unstructured environments. With 48 degrees of freedom and on-board embodied AI, VISAGE perceives, plans, and acts in real time without teleoperation.

Designed from first principles for dexterity, robustness, and deployability, VISAGE is the most capable humanoid we've ever built. And we're just getting started.
